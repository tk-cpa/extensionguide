An extension to file is not an extension to pay tax.

An extension moves the filing deadline. It does not move the payment deadline. The tax is still due by the original due date in every jurisdiction that imposes one, and interest and failure-to-pay penalties accrue on any unpaid balance even under a fully valid extension.
