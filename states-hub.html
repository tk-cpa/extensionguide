An extension to file is not an extension to pay tax.

State-by-state tax filing extension rules, each independently verified against that state's own statute, regulation, or Department of Revenue guidance this session - never copied from another state's rule or assumed to match the federal pattern. Nine states (Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, Wyoming) have no state individual income tax; those pages cover business-entity taxes only.

All 50 states and the District of Columbia are live below, each verified against primary state authority. Local and city-level tax jurisdictions (such as NYC, Philadelphia, and Ohio's RITA/CCA municipalities) are the next build phase - see the master matrix for build status.

City and local tax jurisdictions - live

These localities impose their own income, wage, or earnings tax independent of the state, each with its own extension mechanism. Ohio's page covers all roughly 600 RITA-, CCA-, and self-administered Ohio municipalities under one statewide statute (Ohio Rev. Code §718.05(G)(2)); Michigan's page covers all roughly 24 Michigan cities with a city income tax, including Detroit, under one statewide statute (MCL 141.664(1),(2)).
