Indiana - State Tax Extension

Individuals & Business  •  Conditional-Automatic
Automatic with federal extension90% payment requiredForm IT-9 if no federal extension
← Extension Guide home

An extension to file is not an extension to pay tax.

Indiana automatically honors a federal extension (Form 4868) as long as at least 90% of the state and local income tax due has been paid by the original due date. A taxpayer without a federal extension who still needs more time files Form IT-9 and must meet the same 90% payment standard to avoid penalty.

Get the form: Download Form IT-9 (PDF, forms.in.gov)  •  Extension of Time to File (in.gov)
ItemDetail
Form requiredNone if a valid federal extension was filed and 90% of tax is paid; otherwise Form IT-9
Extension lengthMatches the federal extended due date (typically mid-November for calendar-year filers, since Indiana's extended date runs past the federal one)
Payment thresholdAt least 90% of the state and local income tax due must be paid by the original due date to avoid penalty; interest still accrues on any unpaid balance
AuthorityIC 6-8.1-6-1; IC 6-8.1-10-2.1; IC 6-8.1-10-7
Indiana's county income tax is not a separate local filing - each county's tax is computed on Schedule CT-40 (residents) or CT-40PNR (part-year/nonresidents) and filed as part of the same IT-40 state return, so the extension rule above covers county tax automatically. There is no separate county extension form or process to track.
